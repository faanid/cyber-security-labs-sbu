# Lab 04 — Diffie–Hellman and Man-in-the-Middle Attacks

**Status:** Planned  
**Classification:** Independent practical lab  
**Related homework:** Question 4

## Objective

Model an unauthenticated key exchange and show why key exchange alone does not establish the identity of the other party.

## Planned experiments

- Demonstrate a simplified Diffie–Hellman exchange with safe, small educational parameters.
- Model an intermediary establishing separate keys with two participants.
- Explain how authenticated key exchange and certificate validation bind a public key to an identity.
- Document certificate-chain and hostname-validation assumptions.

## Security notes

Keep the exercise local and conceptual. Do not intercept real traffic or bypass certificate checks on real services.
