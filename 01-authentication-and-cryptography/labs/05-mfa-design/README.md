# Lab 05 — Multi-Factor Authentication Design

**Status:** Planned  
**Classification:** Independent practical lab  
**Related homework:** Question 5

## Objective

Classify authentication factors and design a sample authentication flow that uses distinct factor categories.

## Planned experiments

- Classify knowledge, possession, and inherence factors.
- Compare a password plus SMS OTP with a three-factor design.
- Draw a sample login flow and identify recovery and failure cases.
- Discuss phishing resistance and the limitations of SMS-based OTP.

## Security notes

Do not connect the lab to real accounts or send real SMS messages. Use mocked OTP values and fictional users.
