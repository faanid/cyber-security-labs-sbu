# Course Information

Use this folder for factual information about the course.

Suggested information:
- Official course title (use the exact wording shown by the university).
- Institution: Shahid Beheshti University.
- Completion date or study period, if you want to disclose it.
- A short description of the course scope.
- Certificate, only if you have permission to publish it.

Do not describe the course as a degree or formal Computer Science qualification unless that is what was awarded. Before publishing a certificate, check whether it contains personal identifiers or information you do not want public. Consider redacting student numbers and other sensitive details.

Course slides, handouts, and PDFs may be copyrighted. Do not upload them publicly unless the university or instructor permits redistribution. You can document your own notes and answers instead.
